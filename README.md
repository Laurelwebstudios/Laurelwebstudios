## Who's behind this

**A small team of experts.\*** <sub>\*One point of contact, specialists brought in per job.</sub>

I started this the unglamorous way. I worked through local business websites one at a
time, on a phone, the way a customer would, and wrote down exactly where each one lost me.
The ledger now holds 165 businesses across five counties. Only 42 passed clean. The rest
were quietly handing customers to whoever showed up properly in a search.

That's the gap I work in. Big agencies won't touch Main Street budgets, and template farms
ship the same brochure to everyone. I build locally, for a fixed price.

Before any of this I spent ten years in the United States Marine Corps as an aircrewman and
aviation quality assurance mechanic. The habits carried over: show up early, tell the truth,
finish the job. The day job now is level-two IT support and systems administration, and
your website gets held to that standard: patched and backed up, with someone actually
watching it.

## How the work gets done

- **Free audit first.** Your site, used on a phone like a customer would, written up in plain English.
- **Fast static sites** that load in a blink on a phone in a parking lot.
- **Measured, not guessed.** Speed and accessibility are checked page by page on every change.
- **You stay in charge.** Plain-language editing, so you can change your own hours and photos.
- **One point of contact.** Heavier engineering, photo and video, and marketing copy go to
  contracted specialists, all held to one standard.
